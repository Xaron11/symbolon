# Popover

Non-modal floating content anchored to a button, such as scan details.

**Anatomy:** trigger; floating panel (raised surface, overlay shadow) with free content.

**Accessibility:** the panel is a non-modal dialog and needs a label (for example "Scan details").

**Behaviour:** the trigger toggles it; Escape or a click outside closes it and returns focus to the trigger. Unlike a [Dialog](dialog.md), the page stays usable.
